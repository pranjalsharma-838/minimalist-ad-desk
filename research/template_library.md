# Ad template library: 48 static creative types → what it takes to make each one honestly

The taxonomy (10 families, 48 types) was supplied by the user on 2026-10-03. This file adds, for each type:
- the **image source** it needs;
- the **compliance traps** for a skincare brand in India, with sources in `research/regulatory_sources.md`;
- the **renderer layout** that builds it today, or "new layout needed".

The evidence of which types competitors actually run comes from `research/ad_format_library.md` (74 Meta ads, 10 brands). It will be extended by the Amazon top-seller competitor set (see `research/competitor_map.md` once built).

**Image source codes**
- **PACK+BG**: the real pack shot from the product page, composited by the tool on a background generated by the image model (background only; `lib/image_prompt_check.js` enforces this).
- **PACK**: the real pack shot on a plain or brand-colour background. No generation.
- **REAL PHOTO**: needs real photography: people, skin, application or results. Never AI-generated. The ASCI synthetic-content guideline (Sept 2026) bans AI-made results even when labelled, and requires a label on synthetic people. A generated person holding the real product also fabricates the scene around a real product.
- **LAYOUT**: type, graphics and the pack shot only.
- **MARKETER**: needs facts that aren't on product pages (offer terms, sales numbers, prices of other channels). Placeholders go in, and a human fills them.

## What this means

- **Buildable today, honestly (no new photography): 21 types.** They use the 8 existing layouts with the real pack shot and AI backgrounds: types 1–6, 10*, 12*, 15, 17, 22, 23, 24, 25, 34, 36, 38, 39, 40, 42, 43, 48. (*10 as concern labels only; *12 as frames that stay empty until real photos exist.)
- **Need a new layout but no new photography: 9 types**: 2b badges, 18, 19, 26 (with real reviews on file), 28 (with a sourced number), 32 (FAQ version), 33, 35 (non-personal question), 37.
- **Need real photography or real people: 18 types**: 7, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 16 (result panel), 20, 21, 27, 29, 30, 31, 41, 44, 45, 46, 47. These are briefed to a shoot or a creator. The tool writes the brief and the checked copy, and refuses to generate the image.

The split is the point. The image model is used where it can't misrepresent the product or a result, and nowhere else.
